## Tag & markup notes
- **`<speaker>`**: Direct transliteration via `grc_simple` (`Hermes`, `Athene`, `Zeus`, `Hera`, `Poseidon`, `Aphrodite`, `Kolossos Rhodion`, `Momos`, `Apollon`, `Herakles`, `Hermagoras`, `Timokles`, `Damis`).
- **`<l>` directly under `<p>`**: Preserved in §§1, 31, and 33 (tragic/epic parodies and Apollo's oracle), including §1 where Zeus's first line sits directly in `<p>` before two `<l>` elements.
- **`<div ... resp="gcrane" n="20">`**: Preserved exact `resp` attribute on section 20.
- **`<sp rend="merge">`**: Preserved across multi-section speeches (§§2, 8, 16–18, 20–22, 30, 33–34, 40, 48–49).
- **`<corr>` & `<note>`**: Preserved in situ; `<note>` subtrees kept byte-for-byte identical.
