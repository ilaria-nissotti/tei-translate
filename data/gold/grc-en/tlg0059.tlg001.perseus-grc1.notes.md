## Our Greek vs the reference (followed our Greek)

- A stray "γ" at the end of blocks 62, 75, 104, 115, 125, 134, 197, 218 and 221 (refs 7–15) is
  dropped.
- **4e**: ὑὸν is a typo for υἱὸν ("son").
- The words in `<del>` are translated inside the `<del>`; the reference leaves them out: 4b
  *rightly*, 5b *also*, 5d *outright*, 6 *that*, 7b *For it has been said*, 11e *to show*, 12b
  *both*.
- **12b** (verse of Stasinus): νεικεῖν is translated "rebuke"; the reference reads it differently
  ("name Zeus"). The milestone stays between the two quotations.
