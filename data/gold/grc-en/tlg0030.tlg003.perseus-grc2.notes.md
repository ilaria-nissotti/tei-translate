# Translation notes: `tlg0030.tlg003.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Hyperides, *Against Athenogenes*)

A papyrus text with many editorial restorations. Translated in chunks
(`docs/gold_chunk_workflow.md`), checked with `scripts/gold/check_chunk.py` and
`scripts/gold/check_gold.py`.

## Tags

- `<add>` (restorations) goes around the whole English word. Where one Greek word has two adds,
  the English word is split the same way (the "Atheno<add>g</add>enes" pattern): 5 (Athenogenes,
  then), 17 (overpowered), 22 (belongs), 26 (deceived), 29 (survive, death), 32 (pursued,
  rescued).
- Where an add covers parts of several words, the English span is wide or forced: 3, 8, 14, 16,
  22, 35.
- Letter fragments without brackets are transliterated: inside `<seg>` (3, 18–21, 23, 25, 28–37)
  and bare (30: poi, po, desantes; frag. 36: tou).
- **13**: the `<label>` is "Agreement".

## Translation

- **3**: Χολλίδου is given as "the Cholleidian".
- **11**: a stray ";" after [Dicaeocrates] is dropped.
- **25**: ἐπικρατὴς is read as the name Epicrates.
- **frag. 36**: ἀναλωμάτων λωμάτων is kept as "expenses penses".
