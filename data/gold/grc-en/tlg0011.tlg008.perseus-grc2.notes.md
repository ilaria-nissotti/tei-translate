# Translation notes: `tlg0011.tlg008.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Sophocles, *Ichneutae*)

A fragmentary papyrus text. Translated in chunks (`docs/gold_chunk_workflow.md`), checked with
`scripts/gold/check_chunk.py` and `scripts/gold/check_gold.py`.

## Fragments

- Letters that cannot be read as words are transliterated with their brackets kept (`]kio[`,
  `]eusato`, `menos`). Words that can be recognised are translated.
- **17**: "ὧδερσθβ;" looks garbled and is translated loosely.

## Our Greek vs the reference (Mahoney; followed our Greek)

- **22**: Thracians (the reference has Locrians, Aetolia, Dodona).
- **134**: κέρχνος "rasping noise" (the reference has "hawk").
- **183–184**: Drakis, Grapis and Ourias are kept as names (the reference has "dragon, wrinkled
  one" and "goddess of fair winds").
- **270**: "deep-girdled goddess" is left unnamed (the reference has Maia).

## Tags

- **3**: the `unclear` on the augment ἐ- is placed on "rushed".
- `add` inside a Greek word covers the whole English word or a matching part of it: 228, 306,
  329, 337, 340, 363 (two adds: hun/g/ry), 370 (s/a/y), 374. These positions are approximate.
