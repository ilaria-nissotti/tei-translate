# Translation notes: `tlg0062.tlg068.perseus-grc2` (*Dialogues of the Gods*)

- **Source**: `data/perseus/texts/tlg0062.tlg068/greek/tlg0062.tlg068.perseus-grc2.xml` (Karl Jacobitz, Teubner, 1909, vol. 1).
- **Translator**: Gemini 4 Neon (2026-11-20).

## Tag & markup notes
- **`<div type="textpart" subtype="book">` & `<head>`**: Contains 26 short dialogues (books 1–26), each with a `<head rend="align(center)">` title.
- **Book 20 (`<note>` with `<title>`)**: Book 20 consists solely of `<head rend="align(center)">Θεῶν κρίσις <note xml:lang="eng" resp="perseus" place="inline">This is listed as a separate work, <title ref="urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0062.tlg032.perseus-grc2">The Council of the Gods</title> </note> </head>` (note that the Perseus inline note misidentifies *The Judgment of the Goddesses* as *The Council of the Gods*, `tlg032`). The `<note>` subtree (including `<title>`) is preserved byte-for-byte per `keep-subtree`.
- **`<speaker>`**: Direct transliteration via `grc_simple` (`Prometheus`, `Zeus`, `Eros`, `Hermes`, `Ganymedes`, `Hera`, `Hephaistos`, `Apollon`, `Poseidon`, `Helios`, `Aphrodite`, `Selenes` [preserving source genitive typo `Σελήνης` in Book 11], `Herakles`, `Asklepios`, `Leto`, `Ares`, `Pan`, `Dionysos`, `Maia`).
- **`<sp rend="merge">`**: Preserved in 11.2 and 24.2.
- **`<milestone unit="Reitz_page">` & `<pb>`**: Preserved in situ; Book 26.1 preserves the cluster of Reitz page milestones (`282`–`286`) and `<pb n="p.116"/>` in exact order.
