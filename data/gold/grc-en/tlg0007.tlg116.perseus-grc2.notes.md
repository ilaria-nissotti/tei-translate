# Translation notes: `tlg0007.tlg116.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Plutarch, *To an Uneducated Ruler*)

Translated in chunks (`docs/gold_chunk_workflow.md`), checked with `scripts/gold/check_chunk.py`
and `scripts/gold/check_gold.py`.

## Our Greek vs the references

- **§1**: the Greek has garbled Latin in Greek letters inside a word:
  "περιέ πρινξιπεμ Ἰνεριιδιτιιμ. κοψεν" ("principem ineruditum" inserted into περιέκοψεν). It is
  translated as "cut off" and the inserted letters are dropped.
- **§5**: the reference (`eng4`) follows a different text ("to us he seems", from ἡμῖν).
- **§6**: a stray "5" (a line number) before λογισμὸς is dropped.
- Stray punctuation in the Greek (`;.` `·` `ʼ`) is ignored.
