# Translation notes: `tlg0013.tlg005.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Homeric Hymn 5, *To Aphrodite*)

Translated in chunks (`docs/gold_chunk_workflow.md`), checked with `scripts/gold/check_chunk.py`
and `scripts/gold/check_gold.py`.

## Reference

- No English passage matched by citation. The whole `perseus-eng2` file (Evelyn-White) was used.
  It is close to our Greek and keeps the same line order after l. 86 (89, 90, 87, 88), but its
  line numbers drift: it numbers the necklace line 87 and Anchises' love 88.

## Tags and layout

- Each `<l>` is translated line by line, so the word order is sometimes stiff (ll. 16–17,
  126–127, 164–166).
- The startquote/endquote comments are kept where they are, with no quotation marks added.
- `milestone unit="Para"` stays at the start of its line and `milestone unit="card"` at the end of
  its line, as in the source.
- **l. 223**: "Foolish one!" is a sentence of its own.
- **l. 272**: "their soul" covers both the Nymph and her tree.
