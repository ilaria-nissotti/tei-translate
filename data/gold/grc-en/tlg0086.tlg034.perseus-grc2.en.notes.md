## Policy & Structural Decisions
- **Proper names & numbers**: Translated into standard English (e.g., *Homer*, *Sophocles*, *Euripides*, *Aeschylus*, *Oedipus*, *Orestes*, *Iphigenia*, *Empedocles*, *Agathon*, *Chaeremon*, *Polygnotus*, *Zeuxis*, *Iliad*, *Odyssey*, *Margites*, *ten thousand stades*, *a hundred tragedies*, *twelve*).
- **Grammatical & linguistic examples (chs. 20–22, 25)**: Greek letter names and lexical/dialectal examples occurring in translatable `<p>` and `<q>` elements were rendered in Latin script / English glosses (e.g., `S`, `R`, `G`, `D`, `GR`, `A`, `GRA`, `N`, `Ps`, `X`, `E`, `O`, `I`, `U`; `men`, `etoi`, `de`, `amphi`, `peri`, `sigynon`, `Hermocaicoxanthus`, `poleeos`/`poleos`, `Peleiadeo`/`Peleidou`, `kri`, `do`, `ops`, `meli`, `kommi`, `peperi`, `domaton apo`, `Achilleos peri`) to keep the English translation free of untranslated Greek script outside kept elements.
- **Kept elements**: The single editorial `<note n="GRC">` in 4.20 was preserved byte-for-byte with a space before and no space after.
- **Text-critical markup**: All `<add>`, `<del>` (including the nested `<del>` in 23.7), Bekker `<milestone unit="page">` and `<milestone unit="line">` markers (including `10a`, `14a`, `14b`, `33a`), `<q>`, `<quote>`, and `<l>` elements (with `@part` and `@met`) were preserved in exact structural alignment.
- **Obelized passages**: Cruces (`† ... †`) in Kassel's text (2.6, 3.2, 6.11, 6.26, 7.11, 11.6, 15.11, 18.8, 20.5, 22.9, 24.21, 25.25, 25.30) were translated smoothly following standard scholarly sense without inserting editorial daggers.
