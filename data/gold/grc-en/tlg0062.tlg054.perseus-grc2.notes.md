# Translation notes: `tlg0062.tlg054.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Lucian, *The Dipsads*)

## Edition / reference notes
- Translated `perseus-grc2` (Jacobitz Teubner 1913) rather than `perseus-grc3` (Kilburn Loeb 1959) because `grc2` carries the `<milestone unit="Reitz_page">` and `<lb>` tags needed for gold-set coverage.
- **§4 (`τὸν ἰὸν ταχύς`)**: `perseus-grc2` reads `ταχύς` ("swift in its venom", matching `eng4`); `perseus-grc3` / `eng3` reads `παχύς` ("thick"). Followed our Greek (`swift in its venom`).

## Tag placement notes
- **`<milestone unit="Reitz_page">`**: Placed directly before the English of the following Greek word (`235` before `and`, `236` before `the land`, `237` before `most paradoxical`, `238` before `indeed`, `239` before `for`, `240` before `So`).
- **§6 (`<quote rend="blockquote">`)**: Preserved the 3 `<lb/>` tags between the 4 translated elegiac lines.
