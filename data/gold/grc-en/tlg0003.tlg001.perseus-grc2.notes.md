# Translation notes: `tlg0003.tlg001.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Thucydides, *Histories*)

Translated block by block (chunks of ~1200 Greek words, `scripts/gold/prepare_chunks.py`), each
chunk checked with `scripts/gold/check_chunk.py`, the file assembled and validated with
`scripts/gold/assemble.py` and `scripts/gold/check_gold.py`. Rules: `docs/gold_set_handover.md`; chunk workflow: `docs/gold_chunk_workflow.md`.

## Our Greek vs the references (followed our Greek)

- **2.79.7**: 430 dead.
- **3.75.1**: ten men (a reference has twelve).
- **3.100.2**: five hundred (a reference has six hundred).
- **4.5.1**: Athens.
- **4.13.2**: forty ships.

## Editorial marks

- Words in editorial brackets (`[...]` deletions, `〈...〉` additions) are kept in brackets
  around their English. Bracketed particles with no English equivalent (e.g. `[τε]` in 1.9.3 and
  1.49.6) are dropped, and so is `[τεῖχος]` in 1.64.1.
- The † crux marks are kept around the English in most places (e.g. 1.51.4, 1.57.6, 2.15.4,
  2.65.12, 2.75.3), but were **dropped in two blocks** of book 2 (block indices 1108 and 1222).
  To check.
- Greek words discussed as words are transliterated: *loimos* / *limos* (2.54.3).

## Tags

- The file has few inline tags inside blocks: verse `<l>` inside `<quote>` (Homeric and other
  quotations, e.g. the Homeric hymn in 3.104) and one `<bibl>`. No `pb`, `milestone` or names
  inside blocks.
- Melian dialogue (5.85–5.113): the `<speaker>` abbreviations ΑΘ. / ΜΗΛ. are rendered
  "Ath." / "Mel.".

## Sentences

Sentences are mostly one English per Greek sentence. Where the Greek has no space after a full
stop (`ἀλλήλους.καὶ`, `εἶναι.ἄλλοι`, `προσχωρεῖν.καὶ`) or a stop inside closing quotes, the
English has a normal sentence break although the pipeline's splitter sees one sentence.
