# Translation notes: `tlg0062.tlg001.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Lucian, *Phalaris* I–II)

## Edition / reference disagreements

- **1.10 (`ἂν πικρῶς`)**: Our Greek (`perseus-grc2`, Harmon 1913) adopts Herwerden's conjecture `ἂν πικρῶς` ("so harshly"), matching `perseus-eng2` (Harmon). `perseus-eng4` (Fowler & Fowler 1905) translates the MS reading `ἀδίκως` ("unjustly"). Followed our Greek (`so harshly`), with `<note n="v.1.p.14.n.1">` placed after `so harshly`.
- **1.2 (`ἐκράτησα`)**: Our Greek follows Herwerden in omitting `τῆς ἐπιχειρήσεως` (matching `perseus-eng2`, "succeeded"). `perseus-eng4` includes it ("in my enterprise"). Followed our Greek (`prevailed`), with `<note n="v.1.p.4.n.2">` placed after `prevailed`.
- **2.11–2.12 section boundary**: `perseus-eng2` shifts the sentence `τίνος οὖν ἕνεκα...` into section 11, whereas our Greek and `perseus-eng4` start section 12 with it. Followed our Greek's section boundaries.

## Tag placement notes

- **`<corr>` with trailing punctuation in Greek**: In several places (`1.1` `<corr>ἐστιν·</corr>`, `<corr>παραγαγεῖν·</corr>`; `1.8` `<corr>ἐπικρατήσειν·</corr>`; `1.9` `<corr>μέν,</corr>`, `<corr>ἄν·</corr>`; `2.4` `<corr>ἤνεγκεν·</corr>`), the Perseus Greek `<corr>` tag includes the trailing punctuation mark. In the English gold file, `<corr>` wraps the English word(s) translating the Greek word, leaving English punctuation outside.
- **1.9 (`<corr>μέν,</corr>` and `<corr>ἄν·</corr>`)**: Both are Greek particles without a one-word literal gloss in all phrasings; translated as `<corr>for my part</corr>` (`ἐγὼ μέν`) and `<corr>would</corr>` (`βουλοίμην ἄν`) so each `<corr>` wraps a clean, exact English span.
- **`<pb>` placement**: All mid-paragraph `<pb>` markers are placed at the nearest sentence boundary per `config/policy.yaml` (`empty: {placement: boundary}`): `v.1.p.4` (1.1), `v.1.p.6` (1.3), `v.1.p.8` (1.4), `v.1.p.10` (1.6), `v.1.p.12` (1.7), `v.1.p.16` (1.10, end of paragraph), `v.1.p.18` (1.12, start of paragraph), `v.1.p.20` (1.13), `v.1.p.22` (2.1, at the sentence split), `v.1.p.24` (2.4, end of paragraph), `v.1.p.26` (2.8), `v.1.p.28` (2.9), `v.1.p.30` (2.12). Block-level `<pb>` markers (`v.1.p.2`, `v.1.p.14`) remain untouched in place.
- **No name/transliterate tags**: This Greek file contains no `<name>`, `<persName>`, `<placeName>`, `<rs>`, `<label>`, or `<speaker>` tags, so the transliteration policy was not triggered.
