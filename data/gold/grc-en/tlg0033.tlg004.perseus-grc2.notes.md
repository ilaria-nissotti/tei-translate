# Translation notes: `tlg0033.tlg004.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Pindar, *Isthmian Odes*)

Translated in chunks (`docs/gold_chunk_workflow.md`), checked with `scripts/gold/check_chunk.py`
and `scripts/gold/check_gold.py`.

## Our Greek vs the reference (followed our Greek)

- **4.71**: our Greek speaks of "a third" earlier victory among the boys; the reference leaves this
  out.
- **8.53–55**: the reference reads "he cut down Memnon…"; in our Greek, Memnon and Hector are
  Troy's "sinews".

## Tags

- Line milestones go before the first English word of their line, with the space moved before the
  milestone. In 8.42 and 8.45 that means before the `<q>`.
- `<seg type="sling">` marks a few letters inside a Greek word; the whole English word is wrapped:
  1.31 "dwelling", 3.9 "contests", 4.66 "savoury", 5.33 "Polydeuces", 8.5 "sorrows".
- **6.56**: `<add>ἀν</add>` is the prefix of ἀναγήσασθαι; the whole verb "recount" is wrapped.
- Head blocks: the title is "Isthmian N", names are in their English form, and the race (`seg`)
  is translated.
