## Tag & markup notes
- **`<speaker>`**: Direct transliteration via `grc_simple`: `Φίλων` → `Philon`, `Λυκῖνος` → `Lykinos`. Conventional English forms (`Philo`, `Lycinus`) are used in dialogue prose.
- **`<sp rend="merge">` & `<q rend="merge">`**: Preserved across multi-section narrative speeches and Hetoemocles's embedded letter (§§22–27).
- **`<quote rend="blockquote">` & `<l>`**: Preserved with exact structure, including §25 where Sophocles's first line sits directly in `<quote>` and only the second line is wrapped in `<l>`, and §41 where `<pb n="v.1.p.454"/>` sits inside `<quote>`.
- **`<gap reason="lost" rend="..."/>`**: Preserved in situ in §45 alongside its `<note>`.
- **`<pb>`**: Kept under the exact same parent element as in the source (including `<pb n="v.1.p.444"/>` inside `<q>` in §32).
