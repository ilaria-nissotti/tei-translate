# Translation notes: `tlg0062.tlg043.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Lucian, *The Runaways*)

## Tag & policy notes
- **`<speaker>` transliteration**: Following the team decision to use direct transliteration inside transliterated tags (`grc_simple`), speaker labels are transliterated directly (`Apollon`, `Zeus`, `Philosophia`, `Herakles`, `Hermes`, `Aner`, `Despotes`, `Orpheus`, `Gyne`, `Kantharos`).
- **`<num>` inside `<speaker>`**: Transliterated like the speaker around it (`<num>a</num>`, `<num>b</num>`, `<num>g</num>`): since 2026-11-20 `num` is `translate` in `config/policy.yaml`, and inside a transliterated `<speaker>` the more protective rule (transliterate) wins. (Earlier version: kept in Greek, `num: keep`.) In §§28 and 30 where the source OCR placed bare `β` / `α` inside `<p>` without `<num>`, rendered as `2.` / `1.` so no untranslated Greek remains outside kept elements.
- **Structural quirks preserved**: Empty `<p/>` elements at the end of §§27 and 31, `<sp>` without `<speaker>` in §§20, 21, 28, 32, and `<note>` inside `<speaker>` in §30 are preserved exactly.
- **§30 (`ξεινοδόκον...`)**: Our Greek (`perseus-grc2`, Harmon) assigns this verse to `Gyne` (with `<note n="v.5.p.90.n.2">`), whereas `perseus-eng4` gives it to Hermes. Followed our Greek.
