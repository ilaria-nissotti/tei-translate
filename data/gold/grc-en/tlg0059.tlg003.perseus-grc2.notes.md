# Translation notes: `tlg0059.tlg003.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Plato, *Crito*)

Translated in chunks (`docs/gold_chunk_workflow.md`), checked with `scripts/gold/check_chunk.py`
and `scripts/gold/check_gold.py`.

## Our Greek vs the reference (followed our Greek)

The reference leaves out the words our Greek marks with `<del>`. They are translated inside the
`<del>`:
- **43**: `<del>τῶν ἀγγέλων</del>` → "these <del>messengers</del>".
- **47**: `<del>λόγους</del>` → "<del>words</del>".
- **49**: `<del>ὅπερ καὶ ἄρτι ἐλέγετο</del>`, `<del>γέροντες</del>` ("old").
- **53**: `<del>ἂν</del>` → "would".

## Tags

- Speaker labels ΣΩ. / ΚΡ. are written "Soc." / "Cri.", as in the other Plato files.
- **44**: the `<bibl>` (Hom. Il. 9.363) is copied unchanged.
- Some Stephanus milestones fall on a Greek particle or article that has no English word, so their
  position is approximate: **45c** (after "πολλαχοῦ", before μὲν γὰρ), **47b** ("a <ms/>man"),
  **47d** ("we <ms/>must"), **52c** ("<ms/>for you we…").
