# Translation notes: `tlg0030.tlg001.perseus-grc2.en.xml` (Hyperides, *In Defence of Lycophron*)

A papyrus text with many editorial restorations. Translated in chunks
(`docs/gold_chunk_workflow.md`), checked with `scripts/gold/check_chunk.py` and
`scripts/gold/check_gold.py`.

## Tags

- Many `<add>` elements (restorations) cover only part of a Greek word, or parts of two words. In
  those places the `add` goes around the English of the whole word or words, so the spans are
  approximate. The worst cases are a single word split across two adds: πολλούς as "a great" /
  "many" (frag. 3) and οἰκείων as "members" / "household" (3). Others: κυοῦσαν… ἐξ (frag. 4);
  δίκαιόν ἐστι, where the add also covers "not" (10); τραγῳδίας γράψαι (12).
- `<seg type="noparse">` fragments are transliterated: ros, ton, to, ato (frag. 4).
- The Latin `<head>`s have no Greek and are copied unchanged.

## Translation

- **14**: ὦ inside an `add` is rendered "O men of the jury".
- **13**: our Greek has οὐκοῦν, translated "So then".
